# Biology: Cell Structure and Function

## The Cell
The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are composed of cells.

## Parts of a Cell
1. **Cell Membrane**: The outer boundary of the cell that controls the movement of substances in and out.
2. **Nucleus**: The control center of the cell containing genetic material (DNA).
3. **Cytoplasm**: The jelly-like substance where most cellular activities occur.
4. **Mitochondria**: Known as the powerhouse of the cell, responsible for energy production (ATP).
5. **Ribosomes**: Sites of protein synthesis.
6. **Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER)**: Involved in the transport of proteins and lipids.
7. **Golgi Apparatus**: Modifies, sorts, and packages proteins for secretion.

## Difference between Plant and Animal Cells
- **Cell Wall**: Present in plant cells, absent in animal cells.
- **Chloroplasts**: Present in plant cells for photosynthesis, absent in animal cells.
- **Vacuoles**: Large central vacuole in plant cells, small or no vacuoles in animal cells.
