# Chemistry: Atomic Structure

## Atoms
Atoms are the smallest particles of an element that retain its chemical properties.

## Subatomic Particles
1. **Protons**: Positively charged particles found in the nucleus. The number of protons defines the atomic number.
2. **Neutrons**: Neutral particles found in the nucleus. Protons + Neutrons = Atomic Mass.
3. **Electrons**: Negatively charged particles that orbit the nucleus in specific energy levels or shells.

## Atomic Models
- **Dalton's Model**: Atoms are indivisible spheres.
- **Thomson's Plum Pudding Model**: Atoms consist of a positive sphere with embedded negative electrons.
- **Rutherford's Model**: Most of the atom is empty space with a dense positive nucleus.
- **Bohr's Model**: Electrons move in fixed circular orbits around the nucleus.

## Isotopes
Isotopes are atoms of the same element (same number of protons) but with different numbers of neutrons (different mass numbers).
Example: Carbon-12 and Carbon-14.
